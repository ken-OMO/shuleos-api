# Purpose

This guide explains how parents and guardians use ShuleOS to monitor their child's academic progress, attendance, financial information, and school communication.

The Parent Portal strengthens collaboration between schools and families by providing secure, real-time access to important student information.

---

# Parent Responsibilities

Parents are responsible for:

- Monitoring student progress
- Reviewing attendance
- Tracking fee payments
- Reading school announcements
- Communicating with the school
- Keeping contact information current
- Protecting account credentials

---

# Parent Dashboard

The dashboard provides quick access to:

- Student summary
- Attendance overview
- Academic performance
- Fee balance
- Recent payments
- School announcements
- Notifications
- Upcoming events

Parents with multiple children may switch between student profiles.

---

# Student Profiles

Parents can view:

- Student information
- Admission number
- Class information
- Academic status
- Attendance summary

Parents may only access information for students linked to their account.

---

# Attendance

Parents can review:

- Daily attendance
- Attendance history
- Attendance percentage
- Absence records
- Late arrivals
- Attendance notifications

Parents should contact the school if attendance information appears incorrect.

---

# Academic Progress

Parents may monitor:

- Assignment completion
- Assessment results
- Examination performance
- Progress reports
- Academic trends

Regular review helps identify opportunities for additional support.

---

# Report Cards

Where enabled, parents can:

- View report cards
- Download reports
- Review teacher comments
- Monitor overall performance

Official reports are published according to school policy.

---

# Fee Management

Parents can review:

- Fee balances
- Payment history
- Outstanding invoices
- Payment due dates
- Receipts where available

Payment processing follows the school's financial procedures.

---

# Notifications

Parents receive notifications regarding:

- School announcements
- Attendance updates
- Fee reminders
- Examination schedules
- Academic reports
- Emergency communications

Notification preferences may be configurable.

---

# School Calendar

Parents can view:

- Academic calendar
- School holidays
- Examination periods
- Parent meetings
- School events
- Important deadlines

---

# Communication

Parents may receive or send communications through approved platform features, including:

- School announcements
- Teacher messages
- Administrative notices
- Event notifications

Communication should remain respectful and professional.

---

# Documents

Where enabled, parents may access:

- Report cards
- Fee statements
- School notices
- Official letters
- Student documents

Document availability depends on school policy.

---

# Security

Parents should:

- Keep passwords confidential
- Enable multi-factor authentication where available
- Never share accounts
- Log out from shared devices
- Report suspicious activity immediately

---

# Privacy

Parents may only access information relating to students officially linked to their account.

Student information should not be shared without authorization.

---

# Best Practices

Parents should:

- Check notifications regularly.
- Review attendance frequently.
- Monitor academic performance.
- Pay fees before due dates.
- Keep contact information updated.
- Communicate promptly with the school when concerns arise.

---

# Troubleshooting

Common issues include:

- Login problems
- Missing student records
- Payment visibility
- Notification delays
- Report availability

If problems continue, contact your School Administrator or the school's support office.

---

# Getting Help

If assistance is required:

1. Review this guide.
2. Consult the FAQ.
3. Contact your child's school.
4. Contact platform support if instructed.

The Parent Portal enables families to stay informed, support student success, and maintain effective communication with the school through secure and timely access to important information.

Active parent engagement strengthens the partnership between home and school, helping fulfill the vision of the School in the Clouds.
