# Purpose

This guide helps new users begin using ShuleOS confidently by explaining account access, first-time setup, navigation, and essential platform features.

It serves as the starting point before users begin working within their specific role.

---

# Welcome to ShuleOS

Welcome to **ShuleOS**, the School in the Clouds.

ShuleOS is a cloud-based School Management System that enables schools to manage students, academics, finance, communication, reporting, and administration from one integrated platform.

---

# Before You Begin

Before accessing ShuleOS, ensure you have:

- A supported web browser
- A stable internet connection
- Your username or email address
- Your temporary or permanent password
- Access to your registered email or phone if verification is required

---

# Supported Browsers

For the best experience, use the latest version of:

- Google Chrome
- Microsoft Edge
- Mozilla Firefox
- Apple Safari

Older browsers may not support all platform features.

---

# First Login

To sign in:

1. Open the ShuleOS login page.
2. Enter your username or email.
3. Enter your password.
4. Complete multi-factor authentication (if enabled).
5. Select **Sign In**.

If your credentials are correct, you will be redirected to your dashboard.

---

# Changing Your Password

On first login you may be required to:

- Change your temporary password
- Create a strong password
- Confirm the new password

Choose a password that:

- Is at least 12 characters long
- Contains uppercase and lowercase letters
- Includes numbers
- Includes special characters
- Is unique to your account

---

# Password Security

Never:

- Share your password
- Reuse passwords from other systems
- Save passwords on public computers

Always log out when using shared devices.

---

# Multi-Factor Authentication

If enabled:

1. Enter your username and password.
2. Enter the verification code sent to your device.
3. Complete authentication.

MFA provides additional protection for your account.

---

# Dashboard Overview

After logging in you will see your personalized dashboard.

Depending on your role it may include:

- Announcements
- Notifications
- Attendance summary
- Academic information
- Finance information
- Reports
- Quick actions
- Calendar events

---

# Navigating the Platform

The main navigation menu provides access to modules such as:

- Dashboard
- Students
- Academics
- Attendance
- Finance
- Reports
- Communication
- Administration
- Settings

Available options depend on your assigned role.

---

# Updating Your Profile

Users should review their profile information after first login.

Verify:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Profile picture (if supported)
- Notification preferences

Keep your contact information current.

---

# Notifications

ShuleOS provides notifications for important events including:

- Announcements
- Attendance updates
- Fee reminders
- Examination schedules
- System messages

Review notifications regularly.

---

# Searching

Use the search functionality to quickly locate:

- Students
- Staff
- Classes
- Reports
- Records
- Documents

Search availability depends on user permissions.

---

# Logging Out

Always log out after completing your work.

To log out:

1. Open your profile menu.
2. Select **Log Out**.
3. Close the browser if using a shared computer.

---

# Common First Tasks

New users should:

- Change their password
- Verify profile information
- Review notifications
- Explore the dashboard
- Learn the navigation menu
- Read the guide for their specific role

---

# Troubleshooting First Login

If you cannot log in:

- Verify your username
- Check your password
- Ensure Caps Lock is disabled
- Reset your password if necessary
- Contact your School Administrator
- Contact support if the issue continues

---

Users requiring accessibility assistance should consult the Accessibility Guide.

# Security Best Practices

- Protect your credentials
