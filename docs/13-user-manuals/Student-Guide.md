# Purpose

This guide explains how students use ShuleOS to access academic information, monitor their progress, submit assignments, communicate with teachers, and participate in school activities.

Students should use ShuleOS responsibly while protecting their personal information and following school policies.

---

# Student Dashboard

After signing in, students can view:

- Personal dashboard
- Class schedule
- Attendance summary
- Assignments
- Examination timetable
- Assessment results
- Notifications
- School announcements

Dashboard information is personalized for each student.

---

# My Profile

Students can review:

- Personal information
- Admission number
- Class information
- Contact details
- Profile picture (if enabled)

Students should report incorrect information to the school administration.

---

# Class Timetable

Students can:

- View daily timetable
- Review lesson schedules
- Check classroom locations
- Monitor upcoming classes

Timetable updates are reflected automatically when published by the school.

---

# Attendance

Students can monitor:

- Daily attendance
- Attendance history
- Attendance percentage
- Excused absences
- Attendance notifications

Attendance records are maintained by authorized teachers.

---

# Assignments

Students can:

- View assignments
- Read assignment instructions
- Download learning materials
- Submit completed work
- Review submission status
- Check due dates

Assignments should be submitted before the deadline.

---

# Assessments

Students can access:

- Quizzes
- Continuous assessments
- Practical work
- Projects
- Examination schedules

Assessment details are published by teachers.

---

# Grades and Results

Students can:

- View assessment results
- Review grades
- Monitor academic progress
- Download report cards where permitted

Official results are published according to school policy.

---

# Learning Resources

Depending on school configuration, students may access:

- Course notes
- Learning materials
- Reference documents
- Shared classroom resources

Resources should be used for educational purposes only.

---

# Notifications

Students receive notifications for:

- Assignment deadlines
- Examination schedules
- School announcements
- Attendance updates
- Teacher messages

Notifications should be reviewed regularly.

---

# Communication

Students may communicate through approved platform features, including:

- Teacher announcements
- Class notices
- School notifications

All communication should remain respectful and appropriate.

---

# Calendar

Students can review:

- Academic calendar
- Holidays
- Examination dates
- Assignment deadlines
- School events

The calendar helps students plan their academic activities.

---

# Reports

Students may access:

- Academic reports
- Attendance reports
- Assessment summaries
- Progress reports

Report availability depends on school policy.

---

# Security

Students should:

- Keep passwords confidential
- Never share accounts
- Log out from shared devices
- Report suspicious activity
- Protect personal information

---

# Responsible Use

Students are expected to:

- Use the platform for educational purposes
- Submit original work
- Respect other users
- Follow school policies
- Protect school information

Improper use may result in disciplinary action according to institutional policies.

---

# Best Practices

Students should:

- Check the dashboard daily.
- Complete assignments on time.
- Monitor attendance regularly.
- Review examination schedules.
- Read announcements carefully.
- Keep profile information current.

---

# Troubleshooting

Common issues include:

- Login problems
- Missing assignments
- Timetable updates
- Grade visibility
- Notification issues

If problems continue, contact your teacher or School Administrator.

---

# Getting Help

If you require assistance:

1. Review this guide.
2. Consult the FAQ.
3. Contact your teacher.
4. Contact your School Administrator.
5. Contact platform support if instructed.

Students should use ShuleOS responsibly to support their learning, stay informed about school activities, and monitor their academic progress.
