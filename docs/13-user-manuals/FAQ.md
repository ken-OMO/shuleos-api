# Purpose

This document answers the most frequently asked questions about using the ShuleOS platform.

It provides quick guidance for common user concerns before contacting school administrators or platform support.

---

# General Questions

## What is ShuleOS?

ShuleOS is a cloud-based School Management System (SMS) that helps educational institutions manage students, academics, finance, communication, reporting, and administration from a single platform.

---

## Who can use ShuleOS?

ShuleOS supports multiple user roles, including:

- School Administrators
- Teachers
- Students
- Parents
- Finance Officers
- Registrars
- Librarians
- Support Staff

Each role has access only to authorized features.

---

## Do I need to install software?

No.

ShuleOS is web-based and can be accessed through a supported web browser.

---

## Which browsers are supported?

Use the latest version of:

- Google Chrome
- Microsoft Edge
- Mozilla Firefox
- Apple Safari

Older browsers may not support all features.

---

# Account Questions

## How do I log in?

Enter your username or email address, your password, and complete multi-factor authentication if enabled by your school.

---

## I forgot my password.

Use the **Forgot Password** option if available.

If you cannot reset your password, contact your School Administrator.

---

## Can I change my password?

Yes.

Users should change their passwords regularly and whenever they believe their account may have been compromised.

---

## Can I change my profile information?

Yes.

You can update information that your role is permitted to edit, such as contact details and profile picture where supported.

---

# Student Questions

## How do I view my timetable?

Open the **Timetable** section from your dashboard.

---

## Where can I see my grades?

Grades are available in the **Assessments** or **Results** section after they have been published.

---

## How do I submit assignments?

Open the assignment, review the instructions, upload the required files if applicable, and submit before the deadline.

---

## Why can't I see an assignment?

Possible reasons include:

- The assignment has not yet been published.
- The due date has passed.
- The assignment is not assigned to your class.

Contact your teacher if you believe this is incorrect.

---

# Parent Questions

## How do I check my child's attendance?

Open your child's profile and navigate to the **Attendance** section.

---

## How do I view report cards?

Report cards are available after publication, according to your school's academic calendar and policies.

---

## Can I see information for all my children?

Yes, if multiple students are linked to your parent account.

---

# Teacher Questions

## How do I record attendance?

Open the assigned class, select the attendance option, record attendance for each student, and save your changes.

---

## Can I edit grades?

Yes, if permitted by your school's policies and before grades are finalized or published.

---

## Can I communicate with parents?

Yes, where communication features have been enabled by your institution.

---

# Finance Questions

## How do I record a payment?

Open the student's financial record, select the payment option, enter the payment details, verify the information, and save the transaction.

---

## Can receipts be reprinted?

Yes, if your permissions allow access to receipt history.

---

## Why is a payment missing?

Possible reasons include:

- Payment is still processing.
- Payment was recorded incorrectly.
- Synchronization is pending.

Contact the finance office if necessary.

---

# Reports

## Can I export reports?

Yes, if your role permits exports.

Supported formats may include:

- PDF
- Excel
- CSV

---

## Why is my report empty?

Check:

- Selected filters
- Date range
- Permissions
- Available data

---

# Notifications

## Why am I not receiving notifications?

Verify:

- Notification settings
- Internet connection
- Browser permissions
- School announcements

---

# Security

## Should I share my password?

No.

Passwords should never be shared.

---

## What should I do if I suspect unauthorized access?

Immediately:

- Change your password.
- Notify your School Administrator.
- Report suspicious activity.

---

# Performance

## The system is slow.

Try:

- Refreshing the page
- Checking your internet connection
- Updating your browser
- Trying again later

If the issue continues, contact support.

---

# Accessibility

## Does ShuleOS support accessibility?

Yes.

ShuleOS is designed with accessibility considerations including keyboard navigation, responsive layouts, and readable interfaces.

Refer to the Accessibility Guide for additional information.

---

# Support

## Who should I contact first?

Contact your:

1. Teacher (academic questions)
2. School Administrator (account or school issues)
3. Finance Office (payment issues)
4. Platform Support (technical issues)

---

## What information should I provide when requesting support?

Include:

- Description of the issue
- Error message
- Screenshot if available
- Date and time
- Browser or device information
- Steps to reproduce the issue

---

# Best Practices

Users should:

- Keep passwords secure.
- Review notifications regularly.
- Verify information before saving.
- Log out from shared computers.
- Report problems promptly.
- Keep profile information up to date.
