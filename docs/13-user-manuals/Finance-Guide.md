# Purpose

This guide explains how Finance Officers and authorized administrators use ShuleOS to manage school finances, including fee structures, billing, payments, receipts, financial reporting, and account reconciliation.

Accurate financial management supports institutional sustainability, accountability, and transparency.

---

# Finance Responsibilities

Finance users are responsible for:

- Managing fee structures
- Generating invoices
- Recording payments
- Issuing receipts
- Monitoring outstanding balances
- Processing refunds where authorized
- Producing financial reports
- Maintaining accurate financial records

---

# Finance Dashboard

The finance dashboard provides quick access to:

- Revenue summary
- Outstanding balances
- Recent payments
- Pending invoices
- Financial reports
- Notifications
- Payment trends
- Daily collections

Dashboard information reflects the user's permissions.

---

# Fee Structure Management

Authorized users can:

- Create fee categories
- Define fee schedules
- Configure payment periods
- Assign fees to classes or programs
- Update fee structures for new academic years

Changes should follow the institution's financial approval process.

---

# Student Billing

Finance users may:

- Generate invoices
- Apply fee structures
- View student balances
- Review billing history
- Correct billing errors where authorized

Invoices should be verified before publication.

---

# Recording Payments

Payments can be recorded for:

- Tuition fees
- Registration fees
- Examination fees
- Transport fees
- Boarding fees
- Other approved charges

Every payment should be supported by appropriate documentation.

---

# Payment Methods

Depending on school configuration, payments may include:

- Cash
- Bank transfer
- Mobile money
- Card payment
- Online payment gateway
- Other approved payment methods

Payment methods should comply with institutional financial policies.

---

# Receipts

Finance users can:

- Generate receipts
- Reprint receipts
- Verify receipt history
- Review payment references

Receipts should accurately reflect recorded payments.

---

# Outstanding Balances

Finance users can monitor:

- Student balances
- Overdue invoices
- Partial payments
- Payment plans where supported

Outstanding balances should be reviewed regularly.

---

# Refunds

Where authorized, finance users may:

- Review refund requests
- Process approved refunds
- Record refund transactions
- Maintain supporting documentation

Refunds should follow institutional approval procedures.

---

# Financial Reports

Available reports may include:

- Revenue reports
- Collection summaries
- Outstanding balances
- Payment history
- Fee category reports
- Daily transaction reports
- Financial trends

Reports should be reviewed for accuracy before distribution.

---

# Reconciliation

Finance users should regularly reconcile:

- Recorded payments
- Bank deposits
- Mobile money collections
- Payment gateway transactions
- Outstanding balances

Discrepancies should be investigated promptly.

---

# Notifications

Finance users receive notifications for:

- Successful payments
- Outstanding balances
- Overdue invoices
- Refund requests
- Financial system alerts

Notifications should be reviewed daily.

---

# Search and Filtering

Finance records can typically be searched using:

- Student name
- Admission number
- Invoice number
- Receipt number
- Payment reference
- Date range
- Fee category

Efficient searching improves operational productivity.

---

# Security

Finance users should:

- Protect login credentials
- Verify financial transactions carefully
- Never share accounts
- Log out from shared devices
- Report suspicious financial activity immediately

---

# Data Accuracy

Finance records should always be:

- Complete
- Accurate
- Timely
- Verifiable
- Consistent

Financial corrections should follow approved institutional procedures.

---

# Best Practices

Finance users should:

- Verify invoices before publishing.
- Confirm payments before issuing receipts.
- Reconcile accounts regularly.
- Monitor outstanding balances.
- Review financial reports frequently.
- Protect confidential financial information.

---

# Troubleshooting

Common issues include:

- Missing payments
- Duplicate transactions
- Receipt generation problems
- Invoice discrepancies
- Report generation delays

If problems continue, contact your School Administrator or platform support.

---

# Getting Help

If assistance is required:

1. Review this guide.
2. Consult the FAQ.
3. Contact your School Administrator.
4. Contact platform support if necessary.
