# Purpose

This roadmap defines the long-term strategic direction of the ShuleOS platform.

It identifies the major phases of product evolution and serves as a planning guide for future development while remaining aligned with the vision of delivering a modern, secure, scalable, and cloud-native School Management System.

---

# Vision

ShuleOS aims to become the leading cloud-native education platform for schools by providing an integrated ecosystem for academic, administrative, financial, communication, and analytical operations.

The roadmap is intended to evolve as new technologies, educational requirements, and user feedback emerge.

---

# Guiding Principles

Future development should:

- Deliver measurable value to schools.
- Prioritize security and privacy.
- Preserve backward compatibility where practical.
- Support scalability and multi-tenancy.
- Promote accessibility and usability.
- Encourage automation.
- Remain aligned with the Engineering Constitution.
