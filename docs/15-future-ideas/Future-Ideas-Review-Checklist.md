# Purpose

This checklist establishes the standard process for reviewing future ideas, innovation proposals, research outcomes, and long-term product opportunities before they are accepted into the ShuleOS roadmap.

Its purpose is to ensure every future initiative aligns with the platform vision, engineering principles, business priorities, and educational mission.

---

# Philosophy

Innovation should be intentional rather than accidental.

Every proposed idea should be evaluated objectively, documented consistently, and prioritized according to measurable value instead of personal preference or technology trends.

---

# Review Objectives

Every review should verify that a proposed initiative:

- Supports the ShuleOS vision
- Solves a genuine problem
- Delivers measurable value
- Is technically feasible
- Is operationally sustainable
- Preserves platform quality
- Aligns with engineering standards

---

- [ ] Problem statement documented

- [ ] Aligns with platform vision

Verify:

# Decision

- [ ] Approved

- [ ] Deferred
- [ ] Returned for revision
- [ ] Rejected

# Continuous Improvement

Following each review:

- Update the Innovation Backlog.
- Record lessons learned.
- Refine evaluation criteria.
- Improve documentation.
- Review prioritization decisions.
- Update the Product Roadmap where appropriate.

---

# Best Practices

Review teams should:

- Evaluate ideas objectively.
- Base decisions on evidence.
- Balance innovation with stability.
- Prioritize educational value.
- Document every decision.
- Revisit deferred ideas periodically.

---

# Definition of Done

A future idea review is complete only when:

- Evaluation is completed.
- Risks are documented.
- Dependencies are identified.
- Decision is recorded.
- Action items are assigned.
- Required approvals are obtained.

# Final Standard

Every future idea proposed for ShuleOS should undergo a structured review to ensure it delivers meaningful educational value, aligns with the long-term product vision, and satisfies the platform's standards for security, architecture, operational excellence, and sustainability.

A disciplined review process ensures that innovation strengthens the School in the Clouds platform while preserving the engineering quality and strategic direction established throughout the ShuleOS documentation suite.
